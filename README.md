# Petit Protocol

Offline-first **training and wet-lab aid** for Malawi.kom’s one-day bowel-surgery workshops with clinical officers in Malawi.

**Training and wet-lab aid only. Not for live patient care or unsupervised surgery.**

This copy is a draft for Dr J.J. (Jan) Petit to correct at the 9 December 2026 meeting. It is not signed off for clinical use. Lines he has not confirmed are marked in the app: “Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off”.

Open the app: [Petit Protocol](https://robertsacranietech-ctrl.github.io/petit-protocol/)

After one visit on a connection, add it to an iPhone or Android home screen (browser menu → Add to Home Screen). It then opens with no signal. You can also open `index.html` in a browser with no server.

The day plan, three cow-intestine trays, decision coach, and peri-operative notes follow:

- Malawi.kom annual reports for 2023, 2024, and 2025
- WHO, *Surgical Care at the District Hospital* (small-bowel closure, anastomosis, peritonitis, wound closure)
- Malawi Ministry of Health, *Malawi Standard Treatment Guidelines*, 6th edition, 2023 (§7.11 Peritonitis, §9.7 Typhoid in children, §1.4 Shock)

The Trainer tab keeps the participant register, the four-question workshop evaluation, the wet-lab checklist, and a correction log on the phone (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent to a server.
