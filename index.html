One-day bowel workshop

This page follows the Malawi.kom one-day bowel-surgery workshop for clinical officers, as described in the foundation’s annual reports for 2023, 2024, and 2025. Medical officers and nurses train on the same day.

The 2025 report (Jan Petit, March 2026) describes a day in two parts: an introduction, then supervised practice on fresh cow intestine — oversewing perforations, resection with anastomosis, and making a stoma — and a certificate at the end. The May–July 2025 round recorded 98 professionals.

Hospitals named in that report include St Joseph’s Nguludi, Montfort Nchalo, Trinity Muona, St Peter’s Likoma, and St John’s Mzuzu, with later visits to Phalombe, Embangweni, Mzuzu, Likoma, and Livingstonia.

Tissue

Use fresh cow intestine first. The 2023, 2024, and 2025 reports all describe the practical course on fresh cow intestine. The 2024 report notes that cows are available and that the intestine is close enough to human bowel to practise on.

Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off Pig or goat intestine is only a fallback if cow intestine cannot be found that morning. The annual reports do not describe that fallback.

Animal tissue is dirty skills practice. It is not sterile theatre. Wash hands. Keep flies off the bowel. Dispose of tissue as meat waste unless the hospital says otherwise.

Take-home guide

  1. Resuscitate first. Nil by mouth. Nasogastric tube. Adult antibiotic doses are the Malawi STG peritonitis regimen on the Decide tab. Refer if theatre, anaesthesia, blood, suture, or expertise is missing.
  2. Practise three stitches: two-layer transverse closure, a diverting stoma, and a two-layer end-to-end join.
  3. Water-test every tray. If it leaks or narrows: take the stitches out and redo.
  4. Leave a contaminated skin wound open. Watch the ward afterwards. Ask for help early on the Southern Region surgical WhatsApp network — no personal numbers live in this app.

Sources you can open later: Malawi.kom jaarverslag 2023, 2024, and 2025 (malawikom.org); WHO Surgical Care at the District Hospital, small intestine, anastomosis, peritonitis, and wound chapters; Malawi Standard Treatment Guidelines, 6th edition, 2023, Ministry of Health.

Before and after theatre

Context for the decision, not a prescription chart. Adult notes only. Confirm every live case with the hospital protocol and a supervisor.

Before theatre

  • IV resuscitation. Malawi STG 2023 §7.11 starts peritonitis care with intravenous fluids. §1.4 uses 0.9% sodium chloride or Ringer’s lactate for hypovolaemic shock, and repeats a bolus if required. The sepsis example in §1.4.2 is 30 ml/kg unless the patient is in heart failure. WHO Surgical Care at the District Hospital also starts with normal saline or Ringer’s lactate.
  • Nil by mouth (STG: NPO) and a nasogastric tube (STG §7.11 and the WHO manual).
  • Analgesia (STG §7.11).
  • Adult antibiotics, Malawi STG 2023 §7.11: IV ceftriaxone 2 g every 24 hours for 5–7 days and IV metronidazole 500 mg every 8 hours for 5–7 days. That section says IV antibiotics are indicated for appendicitis, cholecystitis, and bowel obstruction with perforation, and that surgery is indicated for peritonitis.
  • WHO example, different drugs: ampicillin 2 g IV every 6 hours, plus gentamicin 5 mg/kg IV every 24 hours, plus metronidazole 500 mg IV every 8 hours.
  • Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off Which of those regimens he wants drilled in the workshop, and any single fluid volume for every typhoid perforation.
  • Children: do not use the adult doses. STG §9.7 (typhoid in children) gives ciprofloxacin 10 mg/kg by mouth 12-hourly for 14 days, or ceftriaxone 50 mg/kg IV once daily if the child cannot swallow, and says refer if perforation or gut bleeding is suspected.

In theatre — the decision is still the tree below

  • Look along the whole bowel. Count every hole. Note paper-thin bowel next to a hole.
  • A small-bowel perforation, when you repair it: transverse two-layer invaginating closure (WHO). Inner layer through the full thickness of the wall. Outer layer serosa to serosa. WHO also allows a purse-string invaginating suture. Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off on suture material and on running versus interrupted stitches for that repair.
  • Several wounds close together, or ischaemic gut: resect and join end to end in two layers (WHO). The manual’s inner layer is continuous 2/0 absorbable on a half-circle atraumatic needle. The second layer is serosa and muscle, continuous or interrupted.
  • The join should admit the tip of the thumb and finger (WHO).

After theatre

  • Skin. WHO: do not close a contaminated or infected skin wound for primary healing. A potentially contaminated wound is left open and closed as a delayed primary closure after about 2–5 days. Clean-contaminated wounds in that manual are closed after about 48 hours. Close the fascia. Leave dirty skin open.
  • Leak watch. Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off This draft asks teams to look on days 3–5 for new pain, fever, a silent tender abdomen, or dirty fluid from the wound. That window is not a figure printed in the STG or in the WHO peritonitis chapter. Anastomotic leak is named there as one cause of peritonitis.
  • Refer early when theatre, anaesthesia, blood, or suture is missing, or expertise is not available (STG §7.11). Also refer if a patient who has been operated on needs intensive care or another operation.
  • Southern Region surgical WhatsApp consultation network. Use it to ask a surgeon for advice. Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off on the group’s exact name and how a new clinician joins. This app stores no personal phone numbers.

Malawi Standard Treatment Guidelines, 6th edition, 2023, §1.4 and §7.11 and §9.7. WHO, Surgical Care at the District Hospital, chapters on wounds, small intestine, anastomosis, and peritonitis.

Decision coach

Four endings only: repair, resect and join, stoma, or refer. Thresholds Dr Petit has not confirmed are marked.

Three cow-intestine trays

Fresh cow intestine first. Pig or goat only if cow is unavailable that morning. Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off on the fallback.

Do the water test at the end of every tray. The pass rule is a workshop draft, not a signed theatre order.

TRAY 1

Two typhoid ileal perforations

Close two holes, each in two layers, each transversely.

Inner layer. Full thickness of the wall. Invaginate the edge.
Outer layer. Serosa to serosa — a seromuscular, Lembert-type stitch. Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off on the name Lembert, the suture, and running versus interrupted.
1
Rinse the cow intestine. Keep it covered between turns so flies stay off it.
2
On the antimesenteric border, make two small holes to stand in for two typhoid ileal perforations.
3
Close each hole across the bowel, not along it, so you do not turn the hole into a stricture.
4
Inner full-thickness layer, then outer seromuscular layer. WHO Surgical Care at the District Hospital describes this transverse two-layer invaginating closure (a purse-string invaginating suture is the other option in that manual).
5
Water-test. Whether two holes in a living patient are two repairs or one resection is the Decide tab. Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off on that threshold. This tray still practises the two-layer stitch on two holes.
TRAY 2

Diverting stoma

The 2025 report includes making a stoma on fresh cow intestine. Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off on the exact stoma (loop ileostomy or another), the spout, and how the bowel is fixed on the tray.

1
Bring up a loop without twisting the mesentery and without tension.
2
Open it and mature it the way the supervising surgeon shows. Do not invent a new stoma on the day.
3
Fix it to the tray with the method he uses in the workshop. Write his method in the correction log.
4
WHO background only, not his signed typhoid recipe: the district manual describes loop, double-barrel, and end colostomy, and an end ileostomy, mainly for colon injury.
TRAY 3

Resection and end-to-end join

Cut out a short bad segment and join the ends in two layers.

1
Leave a margin of healthy bowel. The cut ends should bleed. WHO: healing of the join depends on a good blood supply.
2
Line the mesentery up. No twist.
3
WHO two-layer anastomosis: inner continuous 2/0 absorbable suture on a half-circle atraumatic needle, full thickness, invaginating the front wall. Second layer takes serosa and muscle. That second layer may be continuous, or interrupted absorbable or non-absorbable.
4
Awaiting Dr. Petit's sign-off Use the suture the district hospital actually has. Confirm size and running versus interrupted with him, and log it.
5
Close the mesenteric window with interrupted absorbable suture. Do not catch the vessels (WHO).
